Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Capture any part of any page, fine-tune the output, and save it exactly where you want — all in a few clicks.

Multiple Capture Modes

Capture the visible viewport, drag to select a custom region with live dimensions, or grab the entire scrolling page — your choice, every time.

Flexible Output Formats

Save as PNG, JPEG, or WebP with configurable quality. Need a PDF? Choose paper size, orientation, margins, scaling, and multi-page splitting.

Save Anywhere

Save straight to your device, to a Google Drive folder, or both at once. Local saving works instantly — no account, no sign-in, no setup.

Filename Templates & Presets

Name files with tokens like {date}, {domain}, {title} and see a live preview. Save entire configurations as presets for one-click capture.

Smart Full-Page Capture

Scrolls and stitches automatically, hides sticky headers so they don't repeat, waits for lazy-loaded images, and restores your scroll position when done.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Power users, rejoice. Alt+Shift+V for visible, Alt+Shift+F for full page, Alt+Shift+R for region — no popup needed.

How It Works

Screenshot in seconds

No complicated setup. Your first capture is four clicks away — and every one after that is just one.

1

Click the icon

Click SnapDock in your toolbar or use a keyboard shortcut to open the popup.

2

Choose your capture

Pick Visible, Area, or Full Page, then select your output format.

3

Hit Capture & Save

Your file lands in Downloads/SnapDock. Your choices are remembered for next time.

4

One click, every time after

Settings are remembered. Next capture? Just one click or shortcut — done.

Privacy & Open Source

Your captures are yours. Period.

SnapDock collects nothing. No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party endpoints, no server component. With local saving, your captures never leave your machine.

The code is open source under the MIT License — inspect it, fork it, improve it. What you capture is your business, not ours.

No Servers

There is no backend. Captures go from your browser to the destination you chose and nowhere else.

🔒

Minimal Permissions

Uses activeTab — only the tab you capture, only when you click. No broad host access, no persistent content scripts.

☁️

Drive Is Optional

Google Drive is off by default. If enabled, it uses a single drive.file scope — SnapDock only sees files it created.

👁️

Fully Auditable

Every line of code is on GitHub. No minified blobs, no hidden network calls. Read it yourself.
